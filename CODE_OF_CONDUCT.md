# Code of Conduct

Qairos contributors must be respectful, constructive and inclusive.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory behavior, doxxing, spam and credential sharing are not acceptable.
Review code and ideas without attacking people. Respect confidential reports and personal information.
These rules apply to issues, pull requests, discussions and project-related communication.

Report concerns privately to support@qairos.tech. Maintainers may remove content, warn participants or restrict access depending on severity.
Reports are handled with discretion; do not include secrets or unnecessary personal information.
